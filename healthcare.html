All Programs MEDICAL OUTREACH

Care brought
closer.

Bringing basic health support, medical outreach and care closer to people who may struggle to access it.

Access to basic healthcare should not depend entirely on someone's circumstances.

Through medical outreach and health-focused support, Mount of Grace helps bring care closer to individuals and communities who may otherwise struggle to access it. Our focus is practical care delivered with compassion and respect.

This is part of our mission to serve communities in Nigeria and the United States.

WHAT WE DO

Inside the programme

Medical outreach

Outreach helps bring basic health support closer to people who find care difficult to access.

Health-focused support

Practical assistance can help people take the next step in looking after their health.

Care with respect

People deserve to be listened to and treated with compassion when they seek help.

Who we serve

  • People who struggle to access basic healthcare
  • Individuals and families facing financial barriers to care
  • Communities where outreach can help bring health support closer

How you can help

  • Support medical outreach. Your donation helps make basic health support possible.
  • Offer professional skills. Health professionals can contact us about opportunities to help.
  • Partner with us. Discuss how your organisation could contribute to upcoming outreach.
