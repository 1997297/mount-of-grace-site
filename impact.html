STORIES OF CHANGE

What a little support can mean

These illustrative examples show how our programmes can help in everyday life.

Nigeria Illustrative example

Room to meet everyday needs

For a widow or elderly person in southern Nigeria, a food parcel can ease the pressure on a limited household budget. Financial assistance, where available, can help with other everyday needs. The support may be modest, but it can make the week a little easier.

Food & Financial Aid for Widows and the Elderly begins with listening to what someone needs.

Nigeria Illustrative example

Support that makes the day easier

In communities across Nigeria, the right mobility aid can make everyday activities more manageable for someone living with a physical disability. Practical assistance should respond to the person's own needs and choices, with respect for their independence.

Support for the Physically Challenged is about useful help and dignity.

United States Illustrative example

A meal and a warm welcome

A household in the United States can face difficult choices when money is tight. Sharing food and essential items during the Christmas season can take some pressure off and make space for a moment of celebration. Our work in the United States carries the same purpose as our work in Nigeria.

Christmas Outreach for the Less Privileged offers practical support and a sense of belonging.

OUR JOURNEY

Seven Years of Change

From direct acts of support to a growing organisation, our purpose has stayed close to the people we serve.

The beginning Simple acts of practical support gave Mount of Grace its starting point: notice a need and help where possible.
The early years Continuing to show up helped the work become more consistent, with food and everyday assistance remaining a part of that care.
As the work grew Outreach gradually broadened to respond to different needs, including Christmas support, mobility assistance, health and learning.
Seven years on Five programmes guide our work in Nigeria and the United States. We're strengthening how we organise that work and share it with the people who support us.
Looking ahead Our next steps are to keep serving, build a stronger structure and understand more clearly how our support helps over time.
