STORIES OF CHANGE

The work in detail

2022 Oguta, Imo State

The classroom that changed a village

When the primary school in Oguta had no classroom for primary 4 through 6, children sat under mango trees — often skipping school when it rained. Mount of Grace partnered with the local Parent-Teacher Association to build a two-classroom block with solar lighting. Enrollment increased 40% within six months.

"My children no longer come home wet and unable to study."

— Parent, Oguta
2023 Okene, Kogi State

Free clinic reaches 400 in one day

Our largest-ever single-day medical camp was held in Okene in March 2023, with 18 volunteer doctors and nurses. Over 400 patients were seen, including 120 children. Common conditions treated: malaria, hypertension, malnutrition, and diabetes. 30 patients were referred for specialist care, with costs covered.

"I had not seen a doctor in six years. This was a gift from God."

— Patient, Okene
2023 Onitsha, Anambra State

Women building businesses together

Through our Women's Empowerment cohort in Onitsha, 45 women completed a 12-week vocational training program. By month six, 28 had launched small businesses — including tailoring, food processing, and hair care. A savings cooperative formed organically from the cohort now holds over ₦2.4 million in pooled savings.

"I am not waiting for anyone to help me anymore. I help myself now."

— Graduate, Onitsha cohort
OUR JOURNEY

Thirteen years of progress

2011 Founded in New York with $8,000 from a group of 12 Nigerian-American professionals.
2013 First medical camp held in Imo State. 80 patients treated in two days.
2015 Education scholarship program launched. 40 students funded in Year 1.
2017 Women's Empowerment program expanded to Anambra and Delta states.
2019 Crossed 5,000 lives impacted milestone. Community development program launched.
2021 Emergency flood relief delivered to 2,000 displaced families in Kogi State.
2023 10,000+ lives milestone. Largest medical camp — 400 patients in one day in Okene.
2024 14,200 lives impacted. Diaspora network grows to 2,800+ donors globally.
