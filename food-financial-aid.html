Practical help for
everyday needs.

Helping widows and elderly people meet everyday needs with dignity through food, essential support and financial assistance.

For many widows and elderly people, even everyday needs can become difficult to meet.

Mount of Grace provides practical assistance that helps ease some of that burden, from food and essential items to financial support where needed. Our aim is simple: to show up with care, respect and meaningful help.

This support is part of our work with people and communities in Nigeria and the United States.

WHAT WE DO

Inside the programme

Food support

Food assistance helps ease the pressure of putting a meal on the table.

Everyday essentials

Practical items can help with needs that are difficult to meet on a limited income.

Financial assistance

Financial support where needed can help widows and elderly people manage everyday costs with dignity.

Who we serve

  • Widows facing financial hardship
  • Elderly people who need help meeting everyday needs
  • People for whom food, essential items or a little financial support can ease immediate pressure

How you can help

  • Donate. Your gift helps make practical assistance possible.
  • Give your time. Contact us about helping with an upcoming outreach.
  • Contribute supplies. Speak with us about the food and essential items currently needed.
