WHO WE ARE

Rooted in faith,
driven by love.

Mount of Grace Foundation serves people and communities in Nigeria and the United States. Over seven years, our work has grown from direct acts of support into five programmes shaped around everyday needs.

We support widows and elderly people, share at Christmas, assist people living with physical disabilities, bring care closer and help young people keep learning. We start by listening to what people need.

OUR PROGRAMMES

Where we focus our work

Food & Financial Aid for Widows and the Elderly

Helping widows and elderly people meet everyday needs with dignity through food, essential support and financial assistance.

Everyday needs can become difficult to meet. We offer food, essential items and financial help where needed, with respect for each person's circumstances. Explore this programme

Christmas Outreach for the Less Privileged

Sharing food, essential items and moments of joy with individuals and families during the Christmas season.

Our Christmas outreach makes room for people who may have little to celebrate with. Sharing food and essential items is one way to offer care and a sense of belonging. Explore this programme

Support for the Physically Challenged

Providing practical support that helps people living with physical disabilities move through everyday life with greater independence and dignity.

The right assistance can make daily activities easier. We offer practical help, including mobility support where available, guided by what each person needs. Explore this programme

Medical Outreach

Bringing basic health support, medical outreach and care closer to people who may struggle to access it.

We help people access basic health support through medical outreach. Care is offered with compassion and respect for the person receiving it. Explore this programme

Education & Youth Development

Supporting young people with learning opportunities, educational resources and encouragement to build stronger futures.

Educational assistance and learning resources can help children and young people keep going. We support opportunities for learning, growth and confidence. Explore this programme
