Education can open doors that circumstances might otherwise keep closed.

Mount of Grace supports children and young people through educational assistance, learning resources and opportunities that encourage growth, confidence and a stronger path forward.

A useful resource or timely encouragement can help a young person stay engaged with learning and see more possibilities ahead.

Inside the programme

Learning resources

Educational materials can help children and young people take part in learning.

Educational assistance

Practical support helps address some of the barriers that make education difficult to continue.

Youth development

Learning opportunities and encouragement help young people build confidence as they consider their next steps.

Who we serve

  • Children and young people who need educational support
  • Learners with limited access to educational resources
  • Young people who would benefit from encouragement and opportunities to grow

How you can help

  • Support learning. Donate towards educational assistance and resources.
  • Contribute materials. Contact us about the learning resources currently needed.
  • Share your experience. Tell us how your skills could support children and young people.
