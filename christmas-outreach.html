All Programs CHRISTMAS OUTREACH FOR THE LESS PRIVILEGED

A season
for sharing.

Sharing food, essential items and moments of joy with individuals and families during the Christmas season.

Christmas should carry a sense of joy and belonging.

Through our Christmas outreach, Mount of Grace reaches individuals and families who may otherwise have very little to celebrate with, sharing food, essential items and the simple reminder that they have not been forgotten.

It is a chance to offer practical help and spend time with people in our communities.

WHAT WE DO

Inside the programme

Food to share

Food support helps individuals and families with everyday needs during the Christmas season.

Essential items

Useful supplies offer practical help at a time when household budgets may be stretched.

Time together

Outreach brings people together and offers a welcome place to share in the season.

Who we serve

  • Individuals facing hardship during the Christmas season
  • Families struggling to meet everyday needs
  • People who would welcome practical support and a sense of belonging

How you can help

  • Support an outreach. Your donation helps provide food and essential items.
  • Volunteer. Contact us about giving your time during the Christmas season.
  • Partner with us. Help organise supplies or contribute to an upcoming outreach.
GET INVOLVED

Someone's story can change because you chose to help.

A meal, medical support, mobility assistance or the chance to keep learning can mean more than we sometimes realise. Your support helps Mount of Grace reach people at moments when a little help can make a real difference.
