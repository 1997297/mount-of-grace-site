ABOUT US

Built on grace,
grown through
community.

Mount of Grace Outreach serves people and communities in Nigeria and the United States. We turn compassion into practical help, with respect for every person we meet.

Our Mission

To provide practical support, care and opportunity to people facing difficult circumstances through food and financial assistance, medical outreach, support for people with physical disabilities, Christmas outreach, education and youth development.

Our Vision

A future where more people have the support, dignity and opportunity they need to move forward, regardless of their circumstances.

7 YEARS OF CARE
OUR STORY

Seven years of
showing up.

Mount of Grace began with direct acts of support for people facing difficult circumstances. A meal, help with an everyday expense or encouragement to keep learning can make a difference when it is needed.

Over seven years, that commitment has grown through the people who give their time and resources. We have continued showing up, learning from the communities we serve and gradually extending our outreach.

Today, our work reaches communities in Nigeria and the United States. As we build a stronger structure for the years ahead, our purpose stays the same: to offer practical help with care and respect.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our core values

Dignity

We treat every person with respect. Needing support never takes away someone's worth or their right to make choices.

Integrity

We take responsibility for the resources entrusted to us and for the promises we make.

Grace

We meet people with kindness, without asking them to prove that they deserve care.

Compassion

We listen to what people are facing and respond with help that is useful to them.

Community

The people we serve know their communities best. Their experience helps guide our work.

Service

We give our time and effort with care, paying attention to the small things that make support meaningful.
