All Programs SUPPORT FOR THE PHYSICALLY CHALLENGED

Support for
greater independence.

Providing practical support that helps people living with physical disabilities move through everyday life with greater independence and dignity.

Living with a physical disability can make everyday activities more difficult, especially when the right support or mobility equipment is out of reach.

Mount of Grace works to provide practical assistance, including mobility support where available, to help people live with greater independence, confidence and dignity.

The person receiving support remains central to this work. Help should respect their choices and what matters to them in everyday life.

What this support means

Mobility
Assistance with movement where available

Dignity

WHAT WE DO

Inside the programme

Mobility support

Access to mobility assistance where available can make daily movement easier.

Practical assistance

Support with everyday needs can help people participate more fully in daily life.

Individual needs

We aim to understand what help would be useful while respecting each person's choices and independence.

Who we serve

  • People living with physical disabilities
  • People who have difficulty accessing appropriate mobility support
  • Individuals who would benefit from practical help with everyday activities

How you can help

  • Donate. Help make mobility support and practical assistance possible.
  • Offer equipment. Contact us first to discuss suitable equipment and current needs.
  • Share your skills. Tell us how your experience could support this work.
