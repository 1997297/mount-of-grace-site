WHAT WE DO

Five programmes.Care in action.

Practical support for people facing difficult circumstances. We serve communities in Nigeria and the United States through five areas of work.

Food & Financial Aid for Widows and the Elderly

Care that helps with the everyday.

Helping widows and elderly people meet everyday needs with dignity through food, essential support and financial assistance.

How this helps

  • Food assistance helps ease the pressure of putting a meal on the table.
  • Practical items can help with needs that are difficult to meet on a limited income.
  • Financial support where needed can help widows and elderly people manage everyday costs with dignity.

Who we serve

Widows facing financial hardship. Elderly people who need help meeting everyday needs.

Read more Support this programme

Christmas Outreach for the Less Privileged

Making room for care at Christmas.

Sharing food, essential items and moments of joy with individuals and families during the Christmas season.

How this helps

  • Food support helps individuals and families with everyday needs during the Christmas season.
  • Useful supplies offer practical help at a time when household budgets may be stretched.
  • Outreach brings people together and offers a welcome place to share in the season.

Who we serve

Individuals facing hardship during the Christmas season. Families struggling to meet everyday needs.

Read more Support this programme

Support for the Physically Challenged

Everyday life, with the right support.

Providing practical support that helps people living with physical disabilities move through everyday life with greater independence and dignity.

How this helps

  • Access to mobility assistance where available can make daily movement easier.
  • Support with everyday needs can help people participate more fully in daily life.
  • We aim to understand what help would be useful while respecting each person's choices and independence.

Who we serve

People living with physical disabilities. People who have difficulty accessing appropriate mobility support.

Read more Support this programme

Medical Outreach

Making basic care easier to reach.

Bringing basic health support, medical outreach and care closer to people who may struggle to access it.

How this helps

  • Outreach helps bring basic health support closer to people who find care difficult to access.
  • Practical assistance can help people take the next step in looking after their health.
  • People deserve to be listened to and treated with compassion when they seek help.

Who we serve

People who struggle to access basic healthcare. Individuals and families facing financial barriers to care.

Read more Support this programme

Education & Youth Development

Helping young people keep learning.

Supporting young people with learning opportunities, educational resources and encouragement to build stronger futures.

How this helps

  • Educational materials can help children and young people take part in learning.
  • Practical support helps address some of the barriers that make education difficult to continue.
  • Learning opportunities and encouragement help young people build confidence as they consider their next steps.

Who we serve

Children and young people who need educational support. Learners with limited access to educational resources.
