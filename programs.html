WHAT WE DO

Six programs.One purpose.

Each program is designed to address a root cause of inequality — not just a symptom. Together they form a comprehensive approach to human flourishing.

📚

Education & Youth

Every child deserves a future.

We believe education is the most powerful lever for generational change. Our education programs provide scholarships, learning materials, tutoring, and mentorship for students from primary school through university.

Impact So Far

  • 2,400+ scholarships awarded
  • 18 partner schools across 4 states
  • 94% scholarship retention rate
  • Career mentorship for 600+ youth

Who We Serve

Children aged 5–22 from low-income households in Imo, Anambra, Kogi, and Delta states.

🏥

Healthcare Access

Dignity in every moment of care.

We run quarterly free medical clinics in underserved communities, provide maternal and child health support, distribute essential medicines, and train community health workers to extend reach between visits.

Impact So Far

  • 9,100+ patients treated
  • 24 free medical camps held
  • 350+ births supported safely
  • 80 community health workers trained

Who We Serve

Families in rural and peri-urban communities with limited access to formal healthcare facilities.

🌸

Women's Empowerment

When women rise, communities rise.

Our women's programs offer vocational training, microfinance, savings cooperatives, and leadership development. We also run survivor support and legal aid services for women facing gender-based violence.

Impact So Far

  • 1,200+ women trained
  • 340+ businesses started
  • $180,000 in microloans disbursed
  • 60% business survival rate at 2 years

Who We Serve

Women aged 18–55, with priority for survivors of violence, widows, and single mothers.

🏗️

Community Development

Infrastructure built to last.

We fund and build essential community infrastructure: clean water boreholes, classroom blocks, community halls, and sanitation facilities — always in partnership with local community councils.

Impact So Far

  • 12 boreholes installed
  • 8 classroom blocks constructed
  • 6 community centers built
  • 30,000+ people with improved water access

Who We Serve

Entire communities, prioritized by infrastructure gaps and local leadership engagement.

🌐

Diaspora Giving

Connecting home, wherever you are.

We help Nigerian Americans channel their resources and skills back home through structured giving, remittance advisory, hometown association partnerships, and professional volunteer programs.

Impact So Far

  • 2,800+ diaspora donors engaged
  • $1.4M raised from diaspora
  • 45 hometown associations partnered
  • 120 professional volunteers placed

Who We Serve

Nigerian diaspora individuals, families, professionals, and hometown associations.

🆘

Emergency Relief

Present when communities need it most.

When floods, droughts, or crises strike, we mobilize rapidly to deliver food, clean water, shelter materials, and trauma support. We partner with state emergency management agencies for coordinated response.

Impact So Far

  • 4 major emergency responses
  • 5,000+ people reached in emergencies
  • 18 tonnes of relief supplies distributed
  • 3 states covered in last 2 years

Who We Serve

Communities affected by natural disasters, displacement, or humanitarian crises in Nigeria.
