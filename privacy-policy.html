How Mount of Grace Outreach collects, uses and protects the information you share with us.

Information we collect

Mount of Grace respects the information you share through this website. We use it to respond to enquiries, coordinate support and keep records of donations.

Information you give us

  • Contact forms. Your name, email address, the type of enquiry, and the message you write.
  • Volunteer and interest forms. Your name, email address, the area of our work you are interested in, and anything you choose to tell us about yourself.
  • Sponsorship enquiries. Your name or organisation, email address, the project you are interested in, and any details you add.
  • Donation receipts. When you submit a receipt, we receive your name, email address, the file you upload and any donation amount and frequency carried through from your selection. A receipt may contain payment details. Please include only the information needed to identify and acknowledge your donation.

Information collected automatically

The services used to operate this website may process basic technical information needed to deliver its pages. Contact us if you have questions about information shared when you visit.

How we use your information

We use the information you give us to:

  • respond to your enquiry, application or sponsorship interest;
  • record and acknowledge donations, and respond to requests for donation documentation;
  • keep you informed about our work where you have asked us to;
  • support the administration of Mount of Grace and its outreach.

Please share only information relevant to the reason you are contacting us.

Who we share it with

We do not intentionally sell your personal information.

Information may be shared where it is needed for:

  • Website services that support the site and handle information submitted through it;
  • Organisational purposes such as responding to enquiries, coordinating volunteering or recording donations;
  • Lawful requests for information, where applicable.

Website form submissions and uploaded donation receipts use Supabase for storage.

How long we keep it

We may keep submitted information for communication, donation records or other legitimate organisational needs. You can contact us with questions about a record or ask for it to be corrected or removed.

Your rights

You can contact us to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • request a correction to information that is wrong or out of date;
  • ask for your information to be removed;
  • let us know that you no longer want to receive updates;
  • ask for a copy of information you have submitted.

Send privacy questions or requests to info@mountofgrace.org.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to handle the information you submit responsibly. Please use the website forms only for information relevant to your enquiry, volunteering interest or donation.

If you are concerned about information you have shared, contact us so we can discuss your request.

Children's privacy

Please contact us before sharing personal information about a child. If you have a concern about a child's information submitted through this website, let us know so we can discuss it with you.

Changes to this policy

Our privacy practices may develop as Mount of Grace and this website grow. This page may be updated to reflect those changes.
